About me

My pathor simply, about me

2021University

Graduated from South Ural State University, School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science. I didn’t go into my field and tried commercial photography instead: weddings, events, celebrations and portraits. That’s when I studied Photoshop in depth, photo editing, light and color, and started training my eye for design.

2022My own business

Started a small business doing on-site upholstery cleaning. I learned to work with people, handle objections and understand customers’ pain points. I handled leads from VK and from a website I built myself on a template. That’s how I got my grounding in marketing. To this day I help businesses process leads and win new clients.

2023UX/UI design

Completed a year-long UX/UI design course, and by 2024 I was already building my first websites that brought clients leads. Since then I’ve created more than 10 projects. For each one I analyzed the niche in the client’s city in depth and called their customers to find the business’s strengths and write precise copy.

2026Vibe coding

Riding the vibe coding wave, I started learning this field. My first project was a web app for my own business, and I saw which problems it could solve for others in my niche.
